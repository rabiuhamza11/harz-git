Journal Test Signal: Baseline Version One of the Durable Change Journal Proof

Purpose of this page in the HARZ Sync mission

This page exists to prove the durable change journal. A real change on a real URL must be detected within the checking interval, journaled with actual hashes, and the journal record must survive restarts.

What the journal must record for every eligible change

The sequence number, detection time, the URL, the previous hash, the new hash, the hash basis, and the delivery state. Detection is not delivery; a change is only eligible when it is journaled durably.

Governing principle of the second internet

When connected, HARZ grows. When disconnected, HARZ continues. This baseline was published to the vault on October 20, 2026.
