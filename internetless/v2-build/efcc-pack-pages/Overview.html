About the EFCC — Overview

Economic and Financial Crimes Commission · stored offline · provenance https://efcc.gov.ng/Overview

Vision

A Nigeria free of economic and financial crimes.

Mission

To eradicate economic and financial crimes through prevention, enforcement and coordination.

Core Values

Integrity · Courage · Professionalism · Collaboration

Our History Through Time

2002 – 2003 · Establishment of EFCC
Established by an Act of the National Assembly on December 12, 2002. Operational activities commenced on April 13, 2003 under Mallam Nuhu Ribadu. Response to pressure from the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) on money laundering.
2004 · Legal Framework Update
The 2002 Establishment Act was repealed and re-enacted as the 2004 Establishment Act, signed into law on June 4, 2004 by President Obasanjo, strengthening the Commission's mandate and operations.
2003 – 2007 · Pioneer Leadership (Mallam Nuhu Ribadu)
Successfully combated 419 fraud (advance fee fraud). Established zonal commands across major cities: Lagos, Port Harcourt, Enugu, Kano, and Gombe. Prosecuted high-profile cases including Fred Ajudua, Emmanuel Nwude, and other notorious fraudsters.
2007 – 2011 · Leadership Transitions
Late Mr. Ibrahim Lamorde (Acting, Dec 2007 – June 2008); Farida Waziri (June 2008 – Nov 2011).
2011 – 2015 · Consolidation Phase (Ibrahim Lamorde)
Confirmed as substantive Chairman in February 2012. Strengthened anti-corruption efforts and international collaborations. Enhanced operational efficiency and case management systems.
2015 – 2020 · Expansion Era (Ibrahim Magu)
Established seven new commands: Ibadan, Kaduna, Benin, Makurdi, Sokoto, Ilorin, and Uyo. Reopened Maiduguri office to strengthen presence in the North East. Intensified the fight against corruption with high-profile prosecutions. Enhanced international cooperation.
2020 – Present · Recent Leadership
Olanipekun Olukoyede (October 2023 – Present); Abdulkarim Chukkol (Acting, June 2023 – October 2023); Abdulrasheed Bawa (March 2021 – June 2023); Mohammed Umar Abba (Acting, July 2020 – March 2021). The EFCC continues to evolve and adapt to new challenges in the fight against economic and financial crimes in Nigeria.
