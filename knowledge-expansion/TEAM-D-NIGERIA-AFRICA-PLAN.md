# HARZ Offline Knowledge Expansion Plan: Nigeria & African Knowledge (Team D)

## 1. Executive Summary & Context

**HARZ** is a Nigerian (Taraba State) sovereign tech ecosystem engineered to deliver offline-first intelligence and knowledge to mobile devices across internet-disconnected and bandwidth-constrained environments. While the baseline HARZ corpus already incorporates Nigerian-language Wikipedias (Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo) and English Wikipedia, raw encyclopedic articles alone do not meet the immediate, high-stakes operational needs of rural and semi-urban communities in Taraba State.

Taraba State presents a unique socio-economic landscape:
- **Geography & Economy:** Stretching from the Benue River valley to the high-altitude Mambilla Plateau (Sardauna LGA), Taraba is a major agricultural hub producing cassava, maize, rice, yam, tea, coffee, and livestock (cattle, goats). 
- **Demographics & Languages:** Lingua francas include Hausa across commercial hubs (Jalingo, Wukari, Bali) and Fulfulde among pastoralist populations, alongside English in schools and government offices.
- **Connectivity Deficit:** Large swathes of rural LGAs suffer from zero or intermittent 2G/3G cellular connectivity, power grid instability, and prohibitive data costs.

This ranked knowledge expansion plan curated by **Team D (Nigeria & African Knowledge)** systematically prioritizes practical, high-utility, locally relevant knowledge collections across seven key domains. The ranking directly reflects utility to three primary disconnected end-user personas in Taraba State:
1. **The Disconnected Farmer / Pastoralist** (needs actionable crop disease diagnosis, soil conservation, livestock health, market storage, and low-cost irrigation advice in Hausa/Fulfulde/English).
2. **The Community School Teacher / Vocational Instructor** (needs offline STEM/technical education guides, interactive simulations, practical trade manuals, and local history/geography).
3. **The Micro-Entrepreneur / Trader / Community Admin** (needs basic accounting, SMEDAN business guides, CAC registration steps, tax basics, and authoritative legal/regulatory references).

---

## 2. Category Assessment & Domain Mapping

This plan covers all seven mandated knowledge categories, ensuring full alignment with West African and Nigerian institutional realities:

1. **Nigerian History, Geography, and Public Institutions:** Local LGA mapping, census/demographic profiles, political history, state government directories, and spatial data for Taraba State.
2. **Agriculture, Crops, Soil Management, Livestock:** Tropical/West African focus including cassava, maize, rice, yam, tea/coffee, cattle care, rotational grazing, soil fertility, and post-harvest storage.
3. **Mathematics, Science, Engineering, and Practical Technical Education:** TVET manuals, low-cost engineering, solar PV installation, rainwater harvesting, carpentry, metalwork, and interactive STEM tools.
4. **Business Management, Accounting, Entrepreneurship, Trade:** SMEDAN micro-enterprise guides, ILO SIYB modules, bookkeeping templates, cooperative management, and market trading.
5. **Publicly Available Nigerian Laws, Regulations, and Government Publications:** Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria 1999 (as amended), Laws of the Federation of Nigeria (LFN), CAC rules, NAFDAC regulations, SON standards, and Land Use Act.
6. **Public Health Education from Authoritative Sources:** NCDC guidelines (Lassa fever, Cholera, Mpox), NPHCDA primary care manuals, WHO clinical guidelines, and community health handbooks.
7. **African Languages and Regional Reference Materials:** Hausa and Fulfulde dictionaries, phrasebooks, parallel corpora, agricultural terminology, and regional linguistic references.

## 3. Comprehensive Ranked Knowledge Collections

Each candidate record includes: Name, URL/Origin, License/Public-Domain Status, Estimated Storage Size, Target Persona Impact, and High-Stakes Notes (mandatory legal/medical/pesticide caveats, publication dates, source attribution, and usage limitations).

### Rank 1: Access Agriculture Farmer Audio/Video & Factsheet Corpus
- **Category:** Agriculture, crops, soil management, livestock
- **URL/Origin:** https://www.accessagriculture.org / https://www.echocommunity.org
- **License / Status:** Creative Commons BY-NC-ND 4.0 / Open Access for non-commercial educational distribution
- **Estimated Size:** ~15.0 GB (Full audio/video) or ~350 MB (Text & Audio factsheet subset)
- **Target Persona Impact:** **Farmer / Pastoralist / Extension Worker.** Delivers practical, peer-to-peer training on tropical crops (cassava, maize, rice, yam), soil erosion prevention, compost making, and livestock management in local languages spoken across Taraba (Hausa, Fulfulde, English).
- **High-Stakes Note:** Agricultural safety note: Agronomic recommendations on pest management must highlight organic/agroecological methods first; where chemical inputs are mentioned, cross-reference with NAFDAC pesticide approval standards.

### Rank 2: Hesperian Health Guides & Community Healthcare Library
- **Category:** Public health education from authoritative sources
- **URL/Origin:** https://hesperian.org / https://wikidoc.fm (Hesperian Foundation)
- **License / Status:** Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 4.0 (for non-commercial educational use in developing nations)
- **Estimated Size:** ~350 MB (Offline HTML & PDF collection)
- **Target Persona Impact:** **Community Health Worker / Teacher / Disconnected Citizen.** Includes *Where There Is No Doctor*, *Where There Is No Dentist*, and *Helping Health Workers Learn*. Invaluable for rural Taraba villages miles away from tertiary hospitals.
- **High-Stakes Note:** **MANDATORY MEDICAL CAVEAT:** Must display revision date (e.g., 2020/2024 revisions), Hesperian source attribution, and the following user disclaimer: *"For educational and first-aid/community health reference only. This content does not constitute medical diagnosis or formal clinical prescription. In severe illness, infection, or medical emergency, transport the patient to the nearest primary health center or hospital."*

### Rank 3: NCDC & NPHCDA National Clinical Guidelines & Epidemic Manuals
- **Category:** Public health education from authoritative sources
- **URL/Origin:** https://www.ncdc.gov.ng / https://nphcda.gov.ng (Nigeria Centre for Disease Control & NPHCDA)
- **License / Status:** Public Domain / Open Government Publications (Federal Republic of Nigeria)
- **Estimated Size:** ~150 MB (PDF repository)
- **Target Persona Impact:** **Rural Clinic Staff / Community Admin / Health Educator.** Authoritative national protocols for managing Lassa fever (endemic in West Africa/Taraba), Cholera outbreaks, Mpox, Yellow Fever, Maternal & Child Health, and Infection Prevention and Control (IPC).
- **High-Stakes Note:** **MANDATORY MEDICAL CAVEAT:** Must state exact NCDC/NPHCDA publication dates (e.g. 2018–2024 manuals) and source attribution. Offline notification: *"Official Nigerian public health guidelines are intended for trained health workers. Offline users are strictly cautioned against administering prescription anti-virals, antibiotics, or invasive treatments without authorized clinical oversight."*

### Rank 4: Practical Action Technical Briefs & Practical Engineering Library
- **Category:** Mathematics, science, engineering and practical technical education
- **URL/Origin:** https://answers.practicalaction.org / https://practicalaction.org
- **License / Status:** Creative Commons BY-NC-ND 4.0 / Open Educational Resource
- **Estimated Size:** ~1.2 GB (800+ technical briefs in PDF/HTML)
- **Target Persona Impact:** **Vocational Instructor / Local Engineer / Craftsman.** Step-by-step practical guides for off-grid solar installation, rainwater harvesting, solar crop drying, low-cost masonry, blacksmithing, small-scale grain milling, and water purification.
- **High-Stakes Note:** Engineering safety note: Electrical solar wiring and structural building designs must carry basic safety disclaimers regarding local building codes, voltage isolation, and load calculations.

### Rank 5: PLAC Laws of the Federation of Nigeria (LFN) & Constitutional Database
- **Category:** Publicly available Nigerian laws, regulations and government publications
- **URL/Origin:** https://lawsofnigeria.placng.org / https://placng.org (Policy and Legal Advocacy Centre)
- **License / Status:** Public Domain (Statutes & Acts of the National Assembly under Nigerian Copyright Act 2022 exceptions for official legal texts) / Open Access
- **Estimated Size:** ~2.5 GB (Searchable text database & PDFs of LFN 2004 + Acts through 2024)
- **Target Persona Impact:** **Businessman / Lawyer / Local Admin / Teacher.** Complete text of the 1999 Constitution (as amended), Criminal Code, Contract Law, Evidence Act, Land Use Act, and Federal Acts.
- **High-Stakes Note:** **MANDATORY LEGAL CAVEAT:** Must carry source attribution ("Laws of the Federation of Nigeria via PLAC"), dataset compilation year (up to 2024), and disclaimer: *"This offline legal text is compiled for general reference and educational purposes only. Statutory provisions are subject to parliamentary amendment and judicial interpretation. This repository does not replace certified official Government Gazettes or formal counsel from a registered legal practitioner."*

## 4. Technical Integration & Offline Deployment Strategy for HARZ

To deliver these 14 ranked collections on low-cost smartphones without internet connectivity, HARZ will employ the following architectural pipeline:

1. **Storage Optimization & ZIM Compression:**
   - Text and static HTML resources (PLAC Laws, SMEDAN modules, Hesperian guides, Agrodok) compressed into optimized `.zim` archives using `zimwriterfs` or Kiwix tools.
   - Total estimated storage footprint for full text + lightweight audio factsheet bundle: **~14.5 GB** (fits easily on a 32GB or 64GB MicroSD card or phone internal storage).
   - High-definition video from Access Agriculture made optional or available as low-bitrate 360p h.264 streams on local HARZ Wi-Fi hotspots.

2. **Offline Local Search & Query Indexing:**
   - SQLite FTS5 (Full-Text Search) combined with lightweight vector embeddings (e.g., MiniLM-L6 quantized) for hybrid keyword + semantic offline search directly on-device.
   - Search indexes pre-built during deployment packaging to avoid device CPU overhead.

3. **Multi-lingual Query Bridge:**
   - Hausa and Fulfulde voice/text queries mapped via Rank 13 parallel glossaries to English indexed content, enabling rural farmers to ask in Hausa (*"Yaya ake maganin kwalliya a masara?"*) and retrieve translated diagnostic solutions from Rank 1 or Rank 8.

4. **Synchronization & Zonal Updates:**
   - Incremental update delta packages distributed via local "sneakernet" (USB OTG drives at LGA extension offices, market day sync stations, and local HARZ hubs in Jalingo and Wukari).

## 5. High-Stakes Risk & Governance Framework

Because offline phones operating in isolated rural settings cannot perform real-time web verification, high-stakes information (Medical, Legal, Biosafety) presents significant safety and liability risks if outdated or misinterpreted.

### Mandatory Metadata Enclosure Architecture
Every medical, legal, regulatory, or chemical record rendered by the HARZ offline phone interface MUST mandatorily inject a **System Metadata Header & Disclaimer Banner** before the content body:

1. **Medical & Public Health Content (Hesperian, NCDC, WHO):**
   - **Header:** `[AUTHORATIVE SOURCE: NCDC / Hesperian Health] | [PUBLICATION DATE: 2024]`
   - **Mandatory Disclaimer Banner:** *"DISCLAIMER: This offline health guide is for informational and educational reference only. It does not constitute medical diagnosis, clinical prescription, or emergency care. If symptoms persist or in severe cases, consult a certified healthcare practitioner or visit the nearest Primary Health Centre immediately."*

2. **Legal & Regulatory Content (PLAC Laws of Federation, NAFDAC, CAC):**
   - **Header:** `[SOURCE: Laws of the Federation of Nigeria via PLAC] | [COMPILATION DATE: 2024]`
   - **Mandatory Disclaimer Banner:** *"DISCLAIMER: This offline text is for educational and general informational reference only. Acts and regulations are subject to legislative amendment and judicial interpretation. This text does not replace certified Government Gazettes or professional legal representation."*

3. **Agricultural Chemical & Biosafety Content (IITA, Agrodok):**
   - **Header:** `[SOURCE: IITA / Agromisa] | [REVISION: 2022]`
   - **Mandatory Disclaimer Banner:** *"DISCLAIMER: Pesticide and fertilizer application rates must strictly follow NAFDAC-approved product label instructions. Wear protective clothing during application. Protect water sources and livestock."*
