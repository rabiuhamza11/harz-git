HARZ Offline Receipt Verifier

Verify a HARZ payment receipt that travelled with no Internet. Runs entirely in your browser — no server, no network.

What this proves: the payer's P-256 signature matches the exact payer, payee, amount and nonce in the receipt, and the receipt has not been altered. What it does not prove: settlement. A receipt is pending until HARZ Chain confirms it. Accept offline payments only up to a trust limit you are willing to carry.
