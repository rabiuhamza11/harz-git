# GAP-5 HANDOFF — OBSERVATION CRITERIA (FROZEN BEFORE THE PERSON)

Frozen: Oct 6, 2026, Dad's ruling, before the first citizen
(Jalingo cooperative) meets the front door.

## THE TEN CRITERIA

1. No coaching — never rewrite their request into HARZ
   language.
2. No task selection by us — they choose what they actually
   need.
3. No explaining the architecture first — the front door
   speaks for itself.
4. No hidden intervention if HARZ struggles.
5. Record the exact user request, verbatim.
6. Record the resulting TaskRecord and receipt.
7. Record the person's outcome: useful answer, useful
   refusal, confusion, correction, or abandonment.
8. Unexpected behavior is evidence, not something to
   immediately patch.
9. Online first.
10. Same person, same task, offline afterward — when the
    field gate is ready.

## THE DISTINCTION

A technically successful TaskRecord is not automatically a
human-successful task. If HARZ closes perfectly and the person
says "I don't understand what this gave me," GAP-5 has found
something real. Likewise, a correct refusal the person
understands can be a success.

## PASS DEFINITION

GAP-5 PASSES when a real person, using their own words and
without being coached, can give HARZ AI a real task and
receive an outcome they can understand, while the complete
TaskRecord remains lawful and inspectable.

The offline run then asks the harder question: does the same
human experience survive when the Internet disappears?

## STANDING RULE

Do not build around the human before the human arrives.
Let the first person expose the next truth.

## WHAT THIS RUNG IS NOT

Not a benchmark. Not an invented customer. Not a demo. The
first instruction HARZ receives that none of us wrote.
