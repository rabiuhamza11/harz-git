# HARZ — Information Infrastructure (Institutional Brief, one page)
_Prepared 31 Oct 2026. Every claim below is deployed and verified, not planned._

## Who we are
HARZ builds information infrastructure: search, knowledge management, digital archives, document integrity and offline information systems. We run the same systems inside our own operations daily (Danfodio Memorial Academy, Bauchi), so every capability listed here has a live reference installation.

## What we deliver

**1. School management suite — live at Danfodio Memorial**
Admission workflow (3-step application with reference numbers), online fees payment (Paystack card, bank transfer, USDT, and wallet options), student portal (results, timetable, assignments, attendance), exam grading with automatic paper generation for 10 subjects, ranking and report-card generation, and a full school website.
_Proof: five live pages, all returning 200 as of 31 Oct 2026. Reference customer: our own school, in daily use._

**2. Document search and archive infrastructure**
Full-text search over large document sets with SHA-256 integrity verification on every retrieved document: a document whose bytes do not match its seal is refused, never served. A verified store of 29,086 documents (96.7 MB) is running in production; a separate sealed corpus of 1,992 documents serves as our integrity reference.
_Proof: reader v1.1 with document-level seal enforcement, battery-verified (46 refused, 154 served, zero false serves)._

**3. Internetless offline information systems**
Systems that keep working when the internet does not: an offline library (pages, images, documents) served from the device, retrievable from any reachable HARZ node over a local network with hash verification, no internet required and no need to store the whole corpus on the phone.
_Proof: offline library deployed; mesh retrieval verified in sandbox (retrieval + hash verification in milliseconds); device proof scheduled on the owner's handsets._

**4. Monitoring and PWA development**
Health monitoring of 60-plus deployed services on a 30-minute cycle, error detection and repair playbooks; installable Progressive Web Apps that run on any modern Android device without app-store friction.

## Engagement models
Annual licence per school with setup and training; fixed-scope projects for archives and search systems; monthly monitoring retainers; and data and AI sovereignty assessments for institutions that must keep documents under their own control.

Practical: built and used in Nigeria, for Nigerian operating conditions — low bandwidth, unstable connectivity and limited budgets included. Honest: we show working systems, not slideware; every reference page in this brief can be opened and tested on the call.
