## Opening (30 seconds)
"Everything I'm about to show you is running today at Danfodio Memorial Academy, our own school in Bauchi. Nothing is a prototype. Open any of these links on your own phone while I talk."

## Scene 1 — School website and programmes (1 minute)
Page: danfodio-memorial.html
Show: nursery through SSS programmes, fees structure, staff directory, events calendar.
Say: "Your school gets a proper web presence that parents can check before they ever call your office. This is the face of the suite."

## Scene 2 — Admission in three steps (2 minutes)
Page: danfodio-admission.html
Show: student information, parent information, review and submit. Point at the auto-generated DMA reference number.
Say: "A parent applies from their phone at night, gets a reference number instantly, and your office receives a complete record instead of a phone call with half the details. No forms lost, no admission book entries to reconcile."

## Scene 3 — Fees payment (2 minutes)
Page: danfodio-fees-payment.html
Show: class selection auto-calculates the correct fees, extras, early-payment discount; four payment routes — card via Paystack, direct bank transfer with reference, USDT, and wallet.
Say: "This is where schools bleed money: unpaid fees and manual reconciliation. Every payment here carries a reference that matches the student. You can see who has paid before the term starts. The discount is applied automatically, so the incentive you promise is the incentive that happens."

## Scene 4 — Student portal (2 minutes)
Page: danfodio-student-portal.html
Show: results view, timetable, assignments, attendance record.
Say: "Parents and students stop calling to ask for results. They check the portal. Your teachers upload once, the whole class sees it."

## Scene 5 — Exam grading and report cards (2 minutes)
Show (live during demo): automatic generation of a 10-subject exam paper, MCQ and theory; class population by enrolment; real-time grading, ranking and report cards.
Say: "Exam week is where teachers lose their evenings. This generates the papers, scores the class, ranks the students and prints the report cards. The headmaster's signature is the last step, not the hundredth."

## Close and ask (1 minute)
"The whole suite runs from one login for your school. Setup includes your classes, your fee structure and your staff accounts, and we train your bursar and exam officer. What I need from you today is simple: pick the module that hurts most — admission, fees or results — and let's run it in your school for one term as a paid pilot. You'll know it works before you commit to the full suite."

## Pricing frame (only if asked)
Per-school annual licence, with setup and training included; pricing depends on enrolment and modules chosen. Pilot terms available for the first schools from the association.

## Demo discipline
1. Always start from the parent's phone, not the admin screen — the buyer feels it instantly.
2. Never show a page that isn't live; every link here is live.
3. Record the demo date, school name and module interest in the DDH ledger the same day.
